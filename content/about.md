## About

{{< lead >}}
Software engineer with 5+ years building production-scale distributed systems —
from high-volume Go microservices serving millions of users, to protocol-layer
infrastructure on the Bitcoin Lightning Network, where I'm a top contributor
to LND (Lightning Network Daemon), the most widely-used implementation. I do
my best work on small, mission-driven teams that move fast without
compromising on quality.
{{< /lead >}}

What a CV won't tell you: I love to build. Quality and fast iteration aren't
in tension for me — they reinforce each other, and clear, direct
communication is what holds them together. I'm equally at home shipping the
code and explaining it on a whiteboard, and I gravitate toward small teams
where everyone owns the outcome.

The current focus happens to be Bitcoin and Lightning, but the things I care
about travel well across domains.

You can grab my CV [here](/CV_Elle_Mouton.pdf).

## Work

### <img src="/img/lightning-labs.png" class="role-logo" alt="Lightning Labs"> Lightning Labs — Lightning Infrastructure Engineer
*2021 — Present*

I joined Lightning Labs in 2021 after deciding in my final year of university
that protocol work was what I wanted to do, and moved onto the Lightning
Network protocol team itself at the end of 2023. Today my work splits across
two areas:

- **Lightning Network protocol & LND (Lightning Network Daemon).**
  Contributions and proposals to the Lightning Network specification, plus
  implementation and review work across LND — the most widely-used Lightning
  implementation. I'm one of the top contributors to the
  [LND repository](https://github.com/lightningnetwork/lnd/graphs/contributors),
  and have also led significant optimisations to the Neutrino Bitcoin
  light-client sync process.
- **Lightning Terminal & Lightning Node Connect.** Led major features across
  the [Lightning Terminal](https://github.com/lightninglabs/lightning-terminal/graphs/contributors)
  daemon and the
  [Lightning Node Connect](https://github.com/lightninglabs/lightning-node-connect/graphs/contributors)
  protocol, where I'm a top contributor to both.

Beyond shipping code, this work involves deep collaboration with the broader
open-source Lightning community — code review, mailing-list spec discussions,
and the last three Lightning Protocol Summits (Tokyo 2024, New York City 2023,
San Francisco 2022).

### <img src="/img/luno.png" class="role-logo" alt="Luno"> Luno — Graduate Software Engineer

This is where I built up production Go experience, got comfortable inside
